Return & Refund Policy

We want you to feel confident with every purchase. If something isn't right, we're here to help.

Our Return Policy

If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may request a return within 7 days of receiving your order, subject to the conditions below.

Return Eligibility

✓

Within 7 Days

Return requests must be made within 7 days of delivery.

✓

Unused Product

Products must be unused and in their original condition.

✓

Original Packaging

Tags, packaging and accessories should be included.

✓

Proof of Purchase

Your order details or receipt may be required.

Non-Returnable Items

  • × Products that have been worn, washed or used.
  • × Products without original tags or packaging.
  • × Products damaged due to customer misuse.
  • × Items returned after the return period.

Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect your returned product, we will notify you about the approval or rejection of your refund.

STEP 01

Request Return

Contact our support team with your order details.

STEP 02

Product Inspection

We inspect the returned product.

STEP 03

Refund

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, please contact us as soon as possible with your order number and photos of the product. We will review the issue and provide an appropriate solution.
