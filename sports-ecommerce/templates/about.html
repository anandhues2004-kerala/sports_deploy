The Story Behind Ecart

Founded in 2026

Ecart started in 2026 with a simple idea: to make online shopping easy, simple, and enjoyable.

The idea was started by Ananthu, the founder and developer of Ecart. With a passion for technology and web development, he started building Ecart as a small project.

What began as a simple idea slowly became a complete e-commerce website where users can explore products, add items to their cart, place orders, and manage their shopping experience.

Every part of Ecart was built with one goal in mind: keeping online shopping simple and user-friendly.
